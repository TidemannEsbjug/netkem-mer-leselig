Norwegian industrial expertise in aquaculture

Netkem AS develops, manufactures and supplies impregnation and coating products, net wash, disinfectants and technical equipment for the aquaculture industry.

Our story

Established in 2001 — built on 30+ years of expertise

NetKem AS is a company that develops, manufactures and supplies products for the aquaculture industry. Our main products are impregnation and coating products for nets, net wash and disinfectants. We also supply technical equipment such as net washing machines and accessories.

NetKem AS was founded in 2001 by Øistein Antonsen, who had worked with these products for many years at Mobil Oil and later ExxonMobil. The expertise from this earlier work is preserved, and new products are continually under development.

The company maintains extensive partnerships with leading aquaculture research institutes, and also in other fields.

Development

Our own products developed for Nordic conditions, with continuous improvements and new variants.

Manufacturing

We manufacture the core wax, polish and wash range ourselves — under Norwegian control.

R&D partnerships

Close collaboration with leading research institutes in aquaculture and other fields.

How we work

Industrial depth over trends. Our products are developed in demanding industries (Mobil Oil, ExxonMobil) and continued with focus on the aquaculture industry's real needs — not short-term market trends.

  • Our products meet EU requirements for health, environment and safety for fish and humans.
  • Key products are BPR-approved (EU chemical regulation) until 2033.
  • Greenline products meet requirements for organic production (ECOCERT, OMRI, EU regulation 2092/91).
  • Coating products meet purity standards from FDA (§ 172.886) and BfR (Germany).
  • We prioritise water-based solutions that are gentle on the net.
