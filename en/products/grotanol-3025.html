Grotanol 3025

Liquid, low-foaming disinfectant for use in the aquaculture industry — full action against bacteria, viruses, yeast and mould.

Disinfection

Low-foaming — broad spectrum of action

Formaldehyde-free PT 2 Water-soluble Low-foaming

Grotanol 3025 is a liquid, low-foaming disinfectant of product type PT 2 — "disinfectants and algaecides not intended for direct application to humans or animals".

Product type BPR PT 2 — Disinfectant/algaecide

Action

Broad spectrum of action against microorganisms

Grotanol 3025 provides full action against bacteria and viruses, as well as yeast and mould. The product also provides full action when disinfecting blood water.

Use areas

Grotanol 3025 is well suited to disinfection of surfaces, equipment and machinery. Because the product foams little, it is also well suited to circulation systems. The product is fully water-soluble, and treated surfaces and tools can be rinsed with water.

Safety

Low toxicity — biodegradable in-use solution

Grotanol 3025 has low toxicity (LD50 oral rat 2079 mg/kg body weight), and the in-use solution is biodegradable. The product is non-flammable, and in solution is normally not irritating to skin and eyes. Grotanol 3025 contains no formaldehyde.

Protective measures: Although Grotanol 3025 has low toxicity in normal solution, the same protective measures as for the concentrate are recommended — including use of rubber gloves. See the separate safety datasheet, which must be read before the product is used.
