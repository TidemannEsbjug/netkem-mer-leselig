Netwax E5 Greenline

Net impregnation for green licences — developed with controlled leaching and an ECOCERT-approved active substance.

Net impregnation for green licences

Optimal protection — within strict environmental requirements

ECOCERT OMRI-listed BPR until 2033 Water-based

Netwax E5 Greenline is developed for net impregnation in green licences. The active substance is dicopper oxide, approved by ECOCERT and listed by OMRI for use in organic farming.

Active substance Dicopper oxide
Certification ECOCERT-approved · OMRI-listed
Approval BPR (EU biocide regulation)

Description

Controlled leaching — well documented

Netwax E5 Greenline ensures optimal protection against fouling — including controlled leaching of the active substance. The product also eases cleaning and prevents the nets from drying out.

Netwax E5 Greenline is formulated to meet health, environment and safety requirements for fish and humans. The product type is well documented with tests carried out at a number of laboratories.

Important on sea wash. Nets treated with biocidal products should not be high-pressure washed at sea, or cleaned by methods that may release impregnation particles into the environment.

Certifications

  • ECOCERT — certifies products for use in organic farming in more than 90 countries.
  • OMRI — Organic Materials Review Institute, USA. Lists products for use in organic food production.
Benefits

What you get with Netwax E5 Greenline

  • Water-based, gentle and safe to use on all common net materials.
  • Prevents fouling.
  • Protects against UV radiation.
  • Keeps nets soft and elastic.
  • Easy to apply, including for re-impregnation.
  • Impregnation can be done indoors.
  • Eases cleaning — fouling and algae release easily under spraying.
  • Nets can be stored after impregnation.
