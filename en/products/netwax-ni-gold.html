Netwax NI Gold

Highly effective net impregnation — our most robust solution for high load.

Premium net impregnation

Optimal protection against fouling

BPR-approved Water-based UV protection Premium

Netwax NI Gold is a highly effective impregnation for nets that reduces fouling, eases cleaning and prevents drying out. Approved under EU chemical regulation (BPR) until 2033.

Product type Net impregnation, water-based
Approval BPR (EU chemical regulation)
Approved until 2033
Use area Net impregnation in aquaculture
Request a quote
Description

Tested for Nordic farming conditions

The product type has undergone extensive testing and environmental documentation — to ensure optimal protection against fouling with controlled leaching of the active substance, and to meet health, environment and safety requirements for fish and humans.

Benefits

Reasons to choose Netwax NI Gold

  • Water-based, gentle and safe to use on all common net materials.
  • Optimal protection against fouling.
  • Easy to apply, including for re-impregnation.
  • Protects against UV radiation.
  • Keeps nets soft and elastic.
  • Impregnation can be done indoors without special precautions or ventilation requirements.
  • Eases cleaning — fouling and algae release easily under spraying.
  • Nets can be stored after impregnation.
