Netpolish NP

Coating and impregnation that protects nets and net materials against mechanical wear, UV and drying out.

Coating & impregnering

Protects the net — season after season

FDA-approved BfR-approved Water-based Organic production

The Netpolish NP series is intended for impregnation and coating of nets and net materials. The series consists of Netpolish NP 30 Standard and Netpolish NP Super. The products provide excellent protection against mechanical wear in for example net washing machines and during spraying and cleaning of nets at sea.

Variants NP 30 Standard · NP Super
Type Vannbasert coating & impregnering
Purity standards FDA § 172.886 · BfR XXV. Empfehlung
Default colour NP 30 supplied red
Flammability Non-flammable as supplied

Description

NP 30 vs NP Super

Netpolish NP 30 produces a soft, smooth film on the net. Netpolish NP Super offers properties that provide optimal protection during cleaning of nets at sea.

The Netpolish NP series can be used in organic production and meets purity standards from FDA and BfR (see Properties). The products are water-based, gentle and safe to use on all common net materials.

Benefits

Why use Netpolish NP

  • Shortens and eases cleaning on land and at sea.
  • Protects the net against mechanical wear.
  • Produces a very even, durable film.
  • Keeps nets soft and elastic.
  • Application can be done indoors without special precautions or ventilation requirements.
  • Nets can be stored after impregnation.
  • Protects the net against UV radiation.
Egenskaper & sertifiseringer

Documented standards

  • Netpolish NP can be used in organic production according to EU regulation 2092/91.
  • Netpolish NP meets the purity standards of the Food and Drug Administration in the USA (FDA § 172.886).
  • Netpolish NP meets the purity standards of Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung in Germany. These apply to products in contact with foodstuffs (BfR XXV. Empfehlung).
  • Netpolish NP is non-flammable as supplied, since water is used as the solvent.
  • Netpolish NP can be used as supplied for impregnation, but may be diluted with water if desired.
  • Netpolish NP can be mixed with the Netwax products.
  • Netpolish NP 30 is supplied red as standard.
