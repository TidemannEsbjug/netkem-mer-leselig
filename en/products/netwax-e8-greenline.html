Heavy-duty net impregnation that ensures optimal protection against fouling. Also protects against UV and helps ease cleaning and prevent drying out.

Active substance Dicopper oxide (copper granulate)
Quality Very pure — approved for organic farming

Description

Newly developed copper granulate — of very pure quality

The active substance is a newly developed granulate containing copper particles of very pure quality — meeting the requirements for use in organic farming.

Dicopper oxide is considered the safest biocide for net impregnation, both for the user and the environment.

Health, environment and safety. Netwax E8 Greenline meets requirements for health, environment and safety for fish and humans, and is approved under EU chemical regulation until 2033.
