Privacy notice

A short overview of how netkem.no handles personal data. We do not track visitors, use no cookies, and only collect what you actively send us via the form or by email.

Last updated: 27 April 2026

Summary

We use no cookies. We do not track visits. There is no analytics, no third-party scripts, no ad networks and no tracking pixels. Fonts, images and video are served from our own server. The only thing we receive from you is what you actively send via the form or by email.

Data controller

Netkem AS, reg. no. 983 922 333
Slalåmveien 1, 1410 Kolbotn, Norway
post@netkem.no · +47 66 80 82 15

What data do we collect?

When you submit an enquiry through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and phone number (required), plus company, subject and message (optional). When you email us directly, we receive what you have written.

We do not log IP addresses, browser fingerprints or visit history. Norwegian law (Electronic Communications Act § 2-7b) and the EU ePrivacy Directive require consent before a website stores information on your device or accesses information already there — typically via cookies, localStorage or similar mechanisms. Since netkem.no neither sets cookies nor uses localStorage or any other client-side storage, and loads no third-party scripts that could do so, there is nothing to consent to. That is why no cookie banner appears here.

Purpose and legal basis

Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry and to follow up any resulting business relationship. The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and performance of, or steps prior to entering into, a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Storage and deletion

Enquiries are deleted when they are no longer needed to respond to or follow up on the matter. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Third parties and data processors

To deliver the website and receive email we use the following data processors:

  • Resend Inc. — sends contact-form email on our behalf. Subject to GDPR-DPA. EU-region servers.
  • Vercel Inc. — provides hosting for the website. Subject to GDPR-DPA. Edge nodes serve static pages locally.
  • Microsoft 365 — handles our email inbox (post@netkem.no).

We share no data with these providers beyond what is technically necessary to deliver their services.

Cookies and local storage

netkem.no sets no cookies and uses no localStorage, sessionStorage or other browser storage. You therefore do not need to consent to anything to use the site.

Tracking and analytics

We use no analytics services (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Matomo, nothing). There are no tracking pixels, no ad networks and no third-party scripts.

Fonts and media

IBM Plex fonts are served from our own server (not Google Fonts). Images and video are also served from our own server. No third party is contacted when you load the site.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (the right to be forgotten)
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Norwegian DPA (datatilsynet.no)

Security

All traffic to netkem.no is encrypted via HTTPS. Submitted form data is sent over encrypted connections to our data processors.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change. Any changes are published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at post@netkem.no.
